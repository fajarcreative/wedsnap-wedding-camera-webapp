## Brand & Style

This design system channels the tactile prestige of high-end letterpress stationery, luxury wedding albums, and the intimate nostalgia of single-use disposable cameras. Crafted for celebration guests and couples, the interface replaces utilitarian event tech with an editorial, romantic keepsake atmosphere.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Editorial Luxury** and **Subtle Tactility**:
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, slow-paced, intimate, and refined. Generous negative space mirrors fine art photography monographs.
- **Visual Tone:** Warm ivory foundations paired with soft champagne metallics and deep charcoal contrast, eliminating cold synthetic whites and harsh digital blacks.
- **Physical Metaphor:** Digital elements mirror pressed cotton stock, debossed foil accents, frosted glass vellum wraps, and mechanical shutter viewfinders.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the tactile quality of heavyweight unbleached paper, foil stamping, and dark ink typography.

### Core Roles
- **Canvas & Background (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm Ivory base layer evoking archival paper.
- **Surface & Containers (`#F5F0EB`):** Muted Cream for cards, modal overlays, and camera housing shells.
- **Primary Foil (`#C5A059`):** Burnished Warm Gold for primary actions, shutter rings, and active camera modes.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D4AF37`):** Brilliant Champagne for celebration milestones, badges, and high-impact micro-interactions.
- **Subtle Highlight (`#E6D5B8`):** Pale Gold leaf for borders, dividers, chip backgrounds, and frosted glass tints.

### Contrast & Boundaries
- **Editorial Ink / Text Primary (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep Charcoal for pristine legibility on ivory.
- **Text Secondary (`#4A4A4A`):** Soft Graphite for film counters, shutter timers, and body copy.
- **Text Muted (`#8C857B`):** Warm Taupe for timestamps, empty states, and metadata.
- **Structural Lines (`#E8E2D9`):** Delicate Warm Border tone reminiscent of debossed margins.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Playfair Display**—a high-contrast editorial serif delivering bridal prestige—with **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a modern humanist geometric sans-serif that ensures effortless clarity for hardware-like controls and camera ergonomics.

- **Editorial Headings:** Set in Playfair Display with loose leading and subtle negative tracking on large scales. Used for couple names, photo counts, and ceremony chapters.
- **Interface & Metrics:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Shutter counters, flash toggles, remaining film rolls, and action items utilize precise medium and semi-bold weights.
- **Stationery Micro-Labels:** Small caps with expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) evoke traditional foil-pressed event programs.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured fluid grid optimized for vertical mobile single-hand capture while expanding into an airy gallery on desktop displays.

- **Mobile Viewport (320px – 640px):** Single-column presentation anchored by a full-height tactile viewfinder container. Canvas margins sit at `1.25rem` (`20px`) with high bottom clearance (`5rem`) to accommodate the physical shutter deck.
- **Tablet (641px – 1024px):** 6-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Controls shift to split-screen ergonomic side rails.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** Centered 12-column presentation capped at `1280px` max-width. Outer margins expand to `3rem` (`48px`) with `2rem` gutters, staging camera previews and album walls like curatorial exhibitions.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card and container padding uses multiples of `0.5rem` (`8px`), favoring `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) and `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to preserve stationery-like breathing space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through diffused warm shadows and frosted translucent overlays, rejecting cold digital dropshadows.

- **Base Layer (Flat):** Paper background (`#FAF8F5`) with no shadow. Structural separation is maintained via delicate `1px` lines tinted with `#E8E2D9`.
- **Card Tier (Vellum Lift):** Cream containers (`#F5F0EB`) float using warm-tinted ambient diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04);`
- **Floating Controls & Viewfinder Panels (Frosted Glassmorphism):**
  Semi-transparent overlays use `background: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and a subtle hairline rim of `1px solid rgba(230, 213, 184, 0.45)`.
- **Modal & Keepsake Reveal Tier:** Deep elevated state for developed film transitions:
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(44, 44, 44, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15);`

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (`2`)** shape vocabulary (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for large elements, and `1.5rem` for major frames). 

This curvature softens the geometric rigidity of digital viewfinders, evoking beveled cardstock, die-cut invitations, and molded vintage camera bodies. Critical operational controls—such as the central shutter trigger, film count badge, and mode toggles—utilize fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`) to invite touch.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Shutter Trigger:** Large circular control (`72px` mobile, `80px` desktop) styled with a double ring: an outer hairline rim in `#C5A059` and an inner ivory core (`#FAF8F5`) that depresses mechanically on `:active` with an inset shadow.
- **Editorial Action Buttons:** Warm ivory or gold-filled rounded rectangles (`rounded-lg`, `height: 48px`). Typography is set in `label-lg`, uppercase, with `0.05em` letter spacing. Outlined variants feature a `1px` border in `#C5A059`.

### Viewfinder & Camera Frame
- The preview lens is framed by a `rounded-xl` window bound with a warm ivory bezel (`#F5F0EB`) and a `1px` interior deboss in `#E8E2D9`. Corner guides are rendered in micro champagne tick marks.

### Film Counter Chip
- Compact pill-shaped element (`rounded-full`) composed of frosted glass (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)`). Houses the remaining shot count (e.g., `18 / 24 EXPOSURES`) set in bold `label-sm` charcoal typography with a warm gold bullet indicator.

### Input Fields
- Understated letterpress fields with unbordered tops and sides, anchored by a `1px` baseline in `#E8E2D9`. Upon focus, the border transitions to `#C5A059` with a subtle amber glow. Labels float in Playfair Display serif italics.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Subtle circular selectors edged in `#E6D5B8`. When selected, they fill with `#C5A059` and feature a centered warm ivory tick or dot.

### Keepsake Album Cards
- Polaroid-proportioned containers featuring a wide bottom margin for handwritten captions. Surfaces are finished in `#F5F0EB` with a `1px` perimeter border in `#E8E2D9`, elevating smoothly on hover with champagne ambient diffusion.
